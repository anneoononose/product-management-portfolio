# Portfolio interview story

## Opening

I wanted to show how I approach ambiguous enterprise problems, not only how I design screens. I selected two domains where poor decisions have measurable business consequences: production reliability and supply-chain disruption.

## SentinelOps

The user problem is not a lack of alerts; it is a lack of early, trustworthy, actionable warning. I narrowed the MVP to capacity forecasting, evidence, and incident handoff. The key tradeoff was depth over breadth: one high-confidence prediction workflow before broad anomaly detection.

## SupplyShield

The user problem is fragmented operational data that prevents teams from connecting disruption to revenue. I designed the SQL model around decisions—stockout risk, revenue exposure, and sourcing concentration—and made query transparency part of the product experience.

## What I would do with a real team

I would interview five users in each primary role, measure current workflows, validate comprehension and task completion with the prototype, and set explicit success and guardrail metrics before committing engineering resources.

## Honest framing

The data and outcomes are simulated. They demonstrate product judgment, analytical thinking, and implementation ability; they are not presented as production results.
